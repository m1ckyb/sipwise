Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 6, 2026

1. Data Collection

SipWise collects email addresses for account creation. We also store health profile data (weight, gender) and alcohol consumption history to accurately calculate BAC estimates.

2. Data Encryption

All sensitive user data (including profile details and drink history) is encrypted at rest using AES-256-GCM. We do not sell your personal data.

3. GDPR & Data Deletion

You have the right to request the deletion of your personal data at any time. You can permanently delete your account and all associated data directly from the application's Profile Settings, which utilizes our secure data deletion endpoint.

4. Third-Party Services

We use Supabase for authentication and database hosting. Push notifications are securely routed via standard browser push services.
